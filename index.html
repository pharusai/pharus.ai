Has this happened to you?

Five things that stop happening.

Pharus keeps one ledger of what you have, what's committed and what's on the way, checks everything against it, and brings you the fix to approve.

01

You were on the phone all day and logged none of it.

A transfer went to Amazon, the co-pack shipped Friday, thirty orders got handled. When you log in, every one is already drafted from your inbox and your platforms. Approve, approve, approve.

02

The stock is in the wrong warehouse.

Orders came in and you have the units, just not where they ship from. Pharus recommends the transfer, routes the order to one location, and drafts the emails to both 3PLs.

03

You placed the production order. You were out of two ingredients.

Pharus checks the bill of materials before the run, finds the vendors you've used, their minimums and the next price break, and drafts the POs.

04

It was selling faster than you noticed.

A SKU outruns its history. Pharus sees it first, recommends the production order early, and spares you the air freight. One brand paid $5,000 to learn this the other way.

05

You don't know which customers make money.

Contribution margin by customer, channel and SKU, from the same ledger that books the orders. No spreadsheet to rebuild every month.

Before you hand anything over

Fair questions.

Is this another system I have to manage?

No. It works with the tools you already use. You approve its work from email or Slack.

Will it do anything without asking me?

No. Pharus drafts; you approve. Every order, PO, payment match and journal entry waits for your tap, with the source document attached. If you later want routine items to post on their own, that's a setting you turn on.

Does it need access to my email?

Only the folders and senders you choose. It reads POs, invoices and remittances. It never sends email as you.

How long does setup take?

Usually within your first week. We set it up with you from your real data and stay through the first live orders.

Does it work with my 3PL?

Yes. ShipVine, ShipHero, Logiwa, ShipBob and RJW Edge are a few we work with. Use someone else? Bring it to your working session.

What does it connect to?

Shopify, Amazon, TikTok Shop, Meta and Google Ads, your 3PLs, EDI, your inbox and your accounting system, to name a few.
