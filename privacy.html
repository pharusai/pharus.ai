Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy explains what Pharus Insights, Inc. (“Pharus”, “we”) collects, what we use it for, and the choices you have. It covers two things:

  • The website at pharus.ai.
  • The Pharus app: the web app at app.pharus.ai, the Pharus API and the Pharus MCP server.

Pharus Insights, Inc. is a Delaware corporation based in Chicago, Illinois.

The website

What we collect

Visit data, through Google Analytics. We use Google Analytics 4, a service from Google, to understand how the site is used. It records:

  • the pages you view, how far you scroll, and clicks on links, including our “Book a working session”, log-in, referral and contact links and which part of the page they were in;
  • your approximate location (country and city), worked out by Google from your IP address. Google Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses;
  • your device type, browser, operating system and language;
  • the site or campaign that sent you here.

This data is tied to a random identifier stored in a cookie, not to your name or email.

What you send us. If you email us or book a session, we receive your name, email address and whatever you write. Bookings are made through Google Calendar appointment scheduling, which is operated by Google.

Hosting. The site is served by our hosting provider, which may log technical data such as your IP address when your browser requests the page, under its own privacy policy.

Consent

If your browser’s time zone is in Europe, a banner asks you to Accept or Decline. Whenever the banner is shown, analytics stays off until you Accept. No analytics cookie is set unless you Accept. Until then Google may still receive limited signals that do not use cookies, such as that a page was opened, which it uses for aggregate statistics. Declining has no other effect on the site. We use your browser’s time zone, not your location, to decide whether to show the banner. Google Analytics is also off by default, until you accept, for visitors Google places in the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland.

Outside those regions, Google Analytics runs without a banner. You can still opt out at any time by blocking the cookies in your browser, by using the Google Analytics opt-out add-on, or by emailing us.

To change a choice you made on the banner, clear this site’s data in your browser and reload the page. Deleting the cookies listed above removes them from your device.

Why we use it

We use visit data to see how many people visit, where they come from, and which parts of the page lead to bookings, so we can improve the site. We use what you send us to reply to you and to set up conversations about Pharus.

How long we keep it

Google Analytics event data is kept for 14 months, then deleted. Emails and booking details are kept as long as needed to deal with your request or our relationship with you, then deleted.

The Pharus app

The Pharus app is used by businesses (“customers”) that sign an agreement with us. It brings a customer’s operational and financial data together: products, inventory, orders, vendors, customers and sales, from the tools the customer connects.

Two kinds of data, two roles

  • Customer data is the business data a customer puts into Pharus or brings in from its connected tools. It belongs to the customer. We process it on the customer’s behalf and only to provide the service, under our agreement with that customer.
  • Account data is what we need to run user accounts: a user’s name, email address, role and profile picture. We are responsible for this data ourselves.

If you are a customer of one of our customers (for example, someone who bought from a brand that uses Pharus) and you have a question about your data, please contact that brand. If you contact us, we will pass your request on to them.

What we collect

Account data. Name, email address, the organizations a user belongs to and their role, and an optional profile picture. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, which stores the email address and password on our behalf.

Usage data. For each minute a user is active, we record how many requests they made, which areas of the app those requests touched (for example, “sales orders”), and whether they came from the web app, the MCP server or an API token. We use this to understand how the app is used and to support customers. We keep it for 13 months. The app itself uses no analytics or advertising trackers.

Customer data entered in the app. Products, materials, inventory, purchase, work, production and sales orders, receipts, transfers, vendors, customers, contacts, notes and the files attached to them.

Customer data from connected tools. When a customer connects a tool, Pharus reads that tool’s data so it can show it in the app. The tools Pharus can connect today are Shopify, Amazon Seller Central, ShipHero, Meta Ads, Google Ads, TikTok Ads, Amazon Ads, Microsoft Ads, Klaviyo, Google Analytics and QuickBooks Online. Depending on the tool this includes orders, line items, products, inventory, shipments, advertising spend and performance, and accounting records. Orders can include the names, addresses and contact details of the people who bought from the customer.

Credentials for connected tools. The tokens that let Pharus reach a connected tool. We store them encrypted, separately from other data, and use them only for that customer’s connection.

Writing into connected tools

Some features write back into a connected tool, for example creating an order in Shopify or ShipHero, or creating invoices, bills or journal entries in QuickBooks Online. Pharus only does this after the customer has connected the tool for that purpose and turned the feature on, and only for the records the feature creates or updates.

Connecting AI assistants and other tools

The Pharus API and MCP server let a customer’s users connect AI assistants or other software of their choice to Pharus, signed in with their own account or token. Those tools can read and change the customer’s data with the same permissions as the user who connected them. Pharus does not send customer data to any AI provider on its own. When a user connects an assistant, the data it reads goes to that assistant’s provider under the customer’s own agreement with that provider.

How we use data

We use customer data only to provide the Pharus app to that customer: to show it, calculate inventory, costs and reports from it, and carry out the actions the customer asks for. We use account data to run accounts, sign users in and send service emails, such as invitations and password resets.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use customer data for advertising.

We may create aggregated statistics from customer data that cannot identify any customer, any of their customers or any person, and use them to improve Pharus. We never attribute them to a customer without its written approval, and we never publish a figure based on fewer than five customers.

Data from QuickBooks Online

When a customer connects QuickBooks Online, Pharus uses the QuickBooks data only to provide the features that customer has turned on: reading accounts, customers, vendors and items to match them with Pharus records, and creating or updating the accounting records the customer has asked Pharus to send. We do not sell QuickBooks data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the service providers listed below. A customer can disconnect QuickBooks at any time from the Connections page in Pharus or from their QuickBooks account. When they do, we delete the QuickBooks tokens we hold.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Credentials for connected tools are encrypted with a separate key. Each customer’s data is kept separate from other customers' data, and access within Pharus is limited to the people who need it to provide the service. If we confirm a security incident affecting customer personal data, we notify the affected customer within 72 hours.

How long we keep it

We keep customer data while the customer’s agreement is active. When an agreement ends, the customer can get a full export of its data, and we then delete our copy, except where the law requires us to keep something. Usage data is deleted after 13 months. Account data is deleted when the account is removed.

For both the website and the app

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, to correct or delete it, to limit or object to how we use it, and you can withdraw consent at any time. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. Email us and we will reply. For data a customer holds about you in Pharus, we will work with that customer to answer your request.

Children

Pharus is for businesses and is not directed at children.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. If a change materially affects how we handle customer data, we will tell our customers before it takes effect.
